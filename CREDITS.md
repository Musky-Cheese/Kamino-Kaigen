# Kamino: credits

A non-commercial, fan-made first-person shooter set during the Battle of Kamino. It's a Kaigen rebuild of
[Musky-Cheese/Kamino](https://github.com/Musky-Cheese/Kamino).

This project is unofficial. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Lucasfilm, Disney or Electronic Arts, and it uses no official game assets.

## Models

Third-party models, used under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). They were decompressed,
rescaled and re-rigged for this game: the B1 and the clone trooper were split into rigid limbs, and the vehicles were
re-oriented and fitted to scale.

The first-person arms and weapon rigs come from the Kaigen Proving Grounds template ("FPS Arms" by djmaesen, CC BY 4.0,
modified). The template credits them on the title screen.

## Code and the rest

The rest is built on the Kaigen engine's Proving Grounds template: the player, weapons, effects, synthesized audio
and HUD. Tipoca City, the storm, the droids' rigs and campaign, and the clone squad are written for this project.
